# Privacy

> Page covering the legal terms of using the product (privacy policy, terms of service, and cookie policy) written clearly and kept current.

## Checklist

- [ ] **Privacy policy** — A clear document explaining what data is collected, how it is used, how long it is kept, and how users can request deletion
      💡 A plain-language summary at the top is an option, to allow most users understand the policy without diving into deeper details unless necessary
- [ ] **Terms of service** — The agreement governing use of the product (user responsibilities, acceptable use, limitation of liability)
- [ ] **Cookie policy** — A clear explanation of which cookies are used, what they do, and how users can control them
      💡 Cookie policies separated from the privacy policy are increasingly what users search for, since they serve different functions and are regulated differently in most jurisdictions
- [ ] **Last updated date** — A clear timestamp on every legal document so users know if they are reading the current version
- [ ] **Version history or changelog** — For products with engaged users, a log of what changed between policy versions — builds trust and reduces enquiries.
      💡 A summary of changes in plain English at the top of an updated policy is far more reassuring than just changing the date.
- [ ] **Contact for legal queries** — A dedicated email or address for data, privacy, or legal enquiries as required by most regulations.
