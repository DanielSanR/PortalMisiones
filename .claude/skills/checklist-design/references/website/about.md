# About

> A page that tells the story of the company — who built it, why, and what they believe.

## Checklist

- [ ] **Origin story** — A genuine account of why the company was founded and the problem it set out to solve
- [ ] **Mission or values** — What the company stands for and how it makes decisions, stated plainly
- [ ] **Team** — The people behind the product (names, roles, and faces) making the company feel human
- [ ] **Milestones or traction** — Key moments in the company's history (founding year, customer count, notable launches) that establish credibility
- [ ] **Investors or backers** — Logos or names of investors, accelerators, or notable backers, where relevant
- [ ] **CTA** — A clear next step e.g. view open roles, read the blog, or try the product
